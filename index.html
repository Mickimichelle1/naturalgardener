All-Natural Pest Control Recipes

🧄 Spray Recipe

Garlic Pepper Spray

A powerful deterrent for soft-bodied insects like aphids, spider mites, and caterpillars — safe for plants and pets.

🧪 Ingredients
  • 1 whole bulb of garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp dish soap (castile)
  • 1 quart warm water

📋 Instructions Blend garlic with ½ cup water. Strain into a quart jar. Add cayenne pepper and dish soap. Dilute with remaining water. Spray directly on affected plants in the early morning.
🌿 Spray Recipe

Neem Oil Fungal & Insect Spray

Tackles aphids, whitefly, powdery mildew, and fungal issues — a homestead staple for the entire growing season.

🧪 Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp cold-pressed neem oil
  • 1 tsp castile soap
  • 1 quart warm water

📋 Instructions Mix soap into warm water first, then add neem oil. Shake well before each use. Spray all leaf surfaces every 7–14 days.
🪨 Barrier Method

Diatomaceous Earth Barrier

Non-toxic fossilized powder that destroys the exoskeletons of slugs, beetles, earwigs, and ants on contact.

🧪 What You Need
  • Food-grade diatomaceous earth (DE)
  • A duster or old flour shaker

📋 Instructions Apply a thin ring of DE around the base of vulnerable plants. Reapply after every rain or heavy dew.
🫧 Spray Recipe

Insecticidal Soap Spray

A gentle yet effective contact spray for scale insects, mealybugs, and aphids — works by suffocating the pest.

🧪 Ingredients
  • 1.5 tsp pure castile soap (unscented)
  • 1 quart cool water

📋 Instructions Mix soap and water gently. Spray directly onto pests. Works best in early morning or evening.
🍶 Weed Control

Vinegar Weed Killer

A fast-acting natural herbicide for paths, driveways, and cracks — kills weeds without harsh chemicals.

🧪 Ingredients
  • 1 gallon white or horticultural vinegar (20% acidity)
  • 1 cup salt
  • 1 tbsp dish soap

📋 Instructions Combine and stir until salt dissolves. Spray directly on weed foliage on a sunny day.
🌻 Prevention

Companion Planting Overview

Strategically planting pest-repelling herbs and flowers is the oldest natural pest control method in the world.

🌱 Top Companion Pairings
  • Basil next to tomatoes — repels aphids
  • Marigolds around the garden edge — deters nematodes
  • Lavender near brassicas — repels cabbage moths

Backyard Poultry Care

🏡 Housing

Setting Up the Perfect Coop

A safe, clean, and comfortable coop is the foundation of healthy backyard chickens.

📐 Key Coop Requirements
  • Minimum 4 sq ft per bird inside the coop
  • At least 10 sq ft per bird in the run
  • 1 nesting box per 3–4 hens
  • Roosting bars 12–18" off the floor
🌾 Feeding

Natural Feeding & Nutrition

Laying hens need a balanced diet. Supplement store-bought feed with forage and kitchen scraps.

🥣 What to Feed
  • 16–18% protein layer pellets
  • Oyster shell for strong eggshells
  • Kitchen scraps: veggies, fruit, cooked grains
🦆 Ducks

Raising Backyard Ducks

Ducks are excellent for pest control, provide rich eggs, and are surprisingly cold-hardy.

🌊 Duck Essentials
  • Always provide deep water for dunking heads
  • Kiddie pools work as a low-cost pond
  • Best beginner breeds: Pekin, Khaki Campbell
💊 Natural Health

Natural Parasite Prevention

Keep your flock free of lice, mites, and intestinal worms using simple, all-natural remedies.

🌿 Natural Treatments
  • Dust bath with wood ash & DE
  • Apple cider vinegar in water
  • Garlic powder in feed
🥚 Egg Production

Maximizing Natural Egg Laying

Understand what drives egg production and how to support your hens through winter.

☀️ Laying Factors
  • Hens need 14–16 hrs of light
  • Stress halts laying quickly
  • Molt is normal, no eggs during molt
🐦 Quail

Raising Coturnix Quail

Quail are ideal for small homesteads — they lay at 6–8 weeks old, are quiet, and need minimal space.

🐣 Quail Basics
  • 1 sq ft per bird
  • Reach laying age in 6–8 weeks
  • Lay 250–300 eggs per year

Growing Perennial Flowers

💜 Perennial

Lavender

Aromatic, drought-tolerant, and irresistible to bees & butterflies.

🌱 Growing Guide
  • Hardiness: Zones 5–9
  • Sun: Full sun (6+ hours)
  • Soil: Well-drained, slightly alkaline
🌸 Perennial

Echinacea (Coneflower)

A stunning prairie native beloved by bees and goldfinches.

🌱 Growing Guide
  • Hardiness: Zones 3–9
  • Sun: Full sun to part shade
  • Bloom time: June through October
🌼 Perennial

Black-Eyed Susan

A cheerful, rugged native wildflower that thrives in difficult conditions.

🌱 Growing Guide
  • Hardiness: Zones 3–10
  • Sun: Full sun preferred
  • Bloom time: July through September
🌺 Perennial

Peonies

Lush, fragrant, and long-lived — a peony plant can bloom for 50+ years.

🌱 Growing Guide
  • Hardiness: Zones 3–8
  • Plant eyes no more than 1–2 inches deep
  • Support heavy blooms with peony rings
🍃 Shade Perennial

Hostas

The king of shade gardens — bold foliage in every shade of green, blue, and gold.

🌱 Growing Guide
  • Hardiness: Zones 3–9
  • Sun: Part to full shade
  • Slugs are the main pest
🔵 Perennial

Salvia (Perennial Sage)

Drought-tolerant spires of violet-blue that hummingbirds and bees absolutely adore.

🌱 Growing Guide
  • Hardiness: Zones 4–9
  • Sun: Full sun
  • Bloom time: May through frost

Intentional Planting

What is Intentional Planting? It's designing your garden with purpose — choosing plants that work together to protect each other, attract beneficial insects, and repel pests naturally.
🤝

Companion Planting Pairs

🌼 Companion Pair
🍅 Tomatoes + 🌼 Marigolds
Repels Nematodes Deters Whitefly

Marigolds release a chemical from their roots that repels root-knot nematodes. Plant French marigolds densely around the tomato bed.

🌱 How to Plant This Pair
  • Use French marigolds (Tagetes patula)
  • Plant 6–12" apart around tomato beds
  • Let marigolds bloom all season
🌿 Companion Pair
🫑 Peppers + 🌿 Basil
Repels Aphids Deters Spider Mites

The volatile oils in basil confuse and repel aphids and spider mites.

🌱 How to Plant This Pair
  • Plant basil 12–18" from each pepper plant
  • Allow basil to flower for beneficial wasps
  • Works with all pepper varieties
🧡 Companion Pair
🥒 Cucumbers + 🧡 Nasturtiums
Trap Crop Repels Beetles

Nasturtiums act as a magnet for aphids, luring them away from cucumbers.

🌱 How to Plant This Pair
  • Plant at ends and corners of cucumber rows
  • Let them sprawl freely
  • Direct sow seeds after last frost
🥕 Companion Pair
🥦 Brassicas + 🌿 Dill
Repels Cabbage Moth Beneficial Hub

Dill's flowers attract tiny parasitic wasps that prey on cabbage loopers.

🌱 How to Plant This Pair
  • Sow dill between cabbage and broccoli
  • Allow dill to bolt and flower
  • Keep away from carrots
⭐ Companion Pair
🍓 Strawberries + ⭐ Borage
Repels Hornworms Boosts Yield

Borage repels tomato hornworms and strawberry pests, and attracts pollinators.

🌱 How to Plant This Pair
  • Direct sow between strawberry plants in early spring
  • Self-seeder — plant once, enjoy for years
💜 Companion Pair
🥬 Kale / Cabbage + 💜 Lavender
Repels Cabbage White Deters Aphids

Lavender's camphor scent disrupts cabbage white butterflies and aphids.

🌱 How to Plant This Pair
  • Plant as a border around brassica beds
  • Use compact varieties like 'Hidcote'
  • Space 18" apart to form aromatic hedge
